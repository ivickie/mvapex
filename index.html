What types of software does MV-Apex Digital Solutions build?
MV-Apex engineers custom business software, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, enterprise and church management systems (such as FPM Global), AI-powered desktop applications (such as GloryBeam), and commercial booking/e-commerce web platforms (such as Susse Wellness Spa).
Where is MV-Apex located and do you work with international clients?
MV-Apex Digital Solutions LTD (RC 9587441) is headquartered in Abuja, FCT, Nigeria, and partners with businesses, startups, and institutions across Nigeria, Africa, Europe, and North America through structured remote delivery.

GloryBeam is a native desktop software application engineered by MV-Apex for AI-assisted church presentations and live media operations—designed to run reliably offline in media booths rather than as a browser-dependent web app.
